Top Attractions

  • Pangong Lake (Ladakh) - a lake that changes colour through the day.
  • Amber Fort (Jaipur) - a hilltop fort with grand courtyards.
  • Alleppey Backwaters (Kerala) - houseboat rides through quiet canals.
  • Baga Beach (Goa) - water sports and beach shacks.
  • Radhanagar Beach (Andaman) - white sand and calm water.

Travel Information

Best time to visit

October to March is good for most of India. Mountains are best from May to September.

Visa

Most foreign visitors need an e-Visa. Apply online at least two weeks before travel.

What to pack

Light clothes, a jacket for the mountains, sunscreen, a power bank and a water bottle.

Getting around

Book trains early, take flights for Ladakh and the islands, and use cabs within cities.
